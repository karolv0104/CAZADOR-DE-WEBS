01 / Positioning

More than investment.
A platform for growth.

JMNII is a privately held global holding company focused on supporting, developing and strengthening businesses through private capital, strategic direction and operational expertise. Headquartered in Miami, with an international presence in the Netherlands and London, the group combines a long-term ownership perspective with the capabilities required to build commercially resilient companies.

At JMNII, we believe the most valuable contribution to a business goes beyond financial resources. We combine capital with strategic direction, operational oversight and access to commercial relationships, supporting businesses as they grow, evolve and enter new markets. Our role is to help turn promising opportunities into stronger, more capable enterprises.

03 / Our Approach

A disciplined approach to opportunity and value creation.

Every business has its own operating reality. Our approach begins with understanding that reality: the market, the people, the capital requirements and the areas where focused support can create the greatest impact.

  1. 01

    Evaluate with care

    We review opportunities against their commercial fundamentals, risk profile, leadership capabilities and potential for sustainable development.

  2. 02

    Align capital and strategy

    We shape the appropriate level of financial and strategic involvement, linking resources to clearly defined business priorities.

  3. 03

    Support the operation

    We help businesses strengthen execution through management oversight, financial discipline, commercial relationships and operational coordination.

  4. 04

    Build for the long term

    We focus on enduring commercial performance, scalable systems and the capabilities required to adapt as markets change.

04 / Capabilities

The resources to invest. The capabilities to execute.

JMNII offers a coordinated group-level platform across capital allocation, ownership strategy and commercial operations. Our capabilities are brought together around the particular needs of each business and relationship.

01

Private capital and holdings

We provide strategic financial support to businesses and opportunities that align with our long-term objectives. Our focus is on thoughtful capital allocation, ownership alignment and building sustainable value over time.

02

Corporate strategy and oversight

We support strategic planning, executive decision-making, budgeting, reporting discipline, performance monitoring and organizational structures to help businesses operate with greater consistency and accountability.

03

Business development and partnerships

We identify commercial opportunities, cultivate relationships, support negotiations and help establish partnerships that can strengthen market positioning, distribution and revenue potential.

04

International expansion and market entry

We support cross-border commercial activity through market coordination, international relationships, supplier engagement and practical planning for entering or serving new territories.

05

Supply chain, sourcing and logistics

Our operating perspective encompasses supplier selection, manufacturing coordination, procurement, warehousing, shipping and distribution workflows, helping businesses connect the commercial plan to physical execution.

06

Brand, intellectual property and commercial assets

We recognize the long-term importance of strong brands and well-managed intangible assets. The group supports brand strategy, commercial licensing coordination, trademark and intellectual-property administration with qualified professional advisers where appropriate.

Opportunity focus

JMNII’s perspective is cross-sector rather than restricted to one industry. Potential areas of interest include consumer brands, apparel and lifestyle, hospitality, health and wellness, international commerce, logistics and selected industrial or energy-related opportunities.

This describes areas of consideration, not a representation of current holdings.

05 / Leadership

Strategic judgment. Direct involvement in execution.

JMNII is guided by executives and advisers with complementary experience in international operations, investment activity, commercial development and private capital. The group’s leadership model combines strategic judgment with direct involvement in execution.

MH CEO

Michael Hobart

Chief Executive Officer

Michael Hobart is Chief Executive Officer of JMNII, responsible for the group’s strategic direction, executive management and coordination of its business interests. He brings broad hands-on experience leading interconnected businesses across the United States and international markets, with expertise spanning corporate operations, financial oversight, commercial development and organizational growth.

In senior leadership roles within the JII group, Michael has overseen multiple operating businesses and complex cross-border initiatives. His responsibilities have included budget planning and financial controls, banking and business administration, commercial negotiations, contract and legal coordination, senior personnel management, product development, manufacturing relationships, distribution infrastructure and international market expansion.

He has led significant operational projects, supported the launch and development of consumer brands, managed multidisciplinary teams and built commercial relationships across different jurisdictions. His leadership is characterized by close attention to execution, financial accountability and the establishment of scalable systems designed to support long-term business performance.

CT COO

Christian Thun

Chief Operating Officer

Christian Thun serves as Chief Operating Officer of JMNII, bringing extensive experience in investment-related business activity, commercial growth and international operations. His background combines strategic business development with expertise in communications, sales leadership and global logistics.

Christian focuses on translating commercial strategy into efficient operating processes and measurable outcomes. His strengths include assessing growth opportunities, improving coordination across business functions, developing commercial relationships and supporting the systems needed for companies to expand across markets.

At JMNII, he works alongside the executive team to support operational performance, business development and the execution of group priorities across domestic and international activities.

JB Advisor

Jay Boateng

Senior Advisor | Private Capital and Portfolio Strategy

Jay Boateng is Senior Advisor to JMNII, contributing extensive experience in private equity and the management of diversified private investment interests. He has worked with high-net-worth individuals and private investment portfolios spanning a broad range of sectors, including energy, restaurants and hospitality, clothing and consumer businesses.

His cross-sector exposure provides an informed perspective on capital opportunities, portfolio development and the practical considerations involved in investing across different industries. He brings a strong understanding of privately held businesses, commercial risk and the importance of aligning investment strategy with long-term objectives.

Jay advises JMNII on private capital strategy, the assessment of selected opportunities and portfolio-level considerations as the group continues to develop its investment and business interests.
