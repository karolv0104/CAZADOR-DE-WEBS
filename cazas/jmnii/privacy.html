Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

This notice explains how JMNII Global Holdings handles the personal information you share with us through this website. We collect as little as possible, use it only to respond to you and never sell it.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by JMNII Global Holdings (“JMNII”, “we”, “us”), a privately held global holding company headquartered in Miami, Florida, with an international presence in London, England and the Netherlands. JMNII is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. You can contact us about privacy at any time at info@jmnii.com.

2. What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us through the contact form:

  • your full name, company, email address and country;
  • the type of inquiry you select and the content of your message.

Please do not include sensitive personal information or confidential commercial details in an initial message. Like any website, our hosting provider also processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type, in order to deliver the site securely.

3. How we use it

We use the information you submit exclusively to review and respond to your business inquiry and, where relevant, to continue the conversation you have started with us. We do not use it for marketing, profiling or automated decision-making.

4. We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes, including for cross-context behavioural advertising as described in U.S. state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

5. Legal basis (UK and EU)

Where the UK GDPR or the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process your information on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries addressed to us, or to take steps at your request before entering into a business relationship.

6. Service providers

We rely on a small number of carefully selected providers who act on our instructions and may only use your information to provide their service to us:

  • website hosting and security (Netlify);
  • delivery of contact form messages to our inbox (Web3Forms);
  • business email (Google Workspace);
  • the website typeface (Google Fonts), which receives your device’s IP address in order to deliver the font files.

7. International transfers

Our leadership and some of our providers operate in the United States, the United Kingdom and the European Union, so your information may be accessed in more than one of these jurisdictions. Where information protected by UK or EU law is transferred, we rely on safeguards recognised by that law, such as adequacy decisions, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where applicable, or standard contractual clauses.

8. How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information only for as long as is necessary to handle your inquiry and any relationship that follows from it, and then delete it securely.

9. Security

This website is served exclusively over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and uses strict security settings that limit which services the site can communicate with. Access to inquiries is restricted to the people at JMNII who need it to respond.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. It only uses two essential settings stored in your own browser:

  • your choice on our privacy banner (“Accept” or “Decline”), kept in local storage so the banner is not shown again;
  • a setting kept in session storage so the opening animation is shown only once per visit, removed when you close the tab.

Because these settings are strictly necessary and contain no personal information, choosing “Decline” does not limit how the website works. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw any consent you have given. Residents of certain U.S. states have similar rights to know, correct and delete their information and to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do not sell or share it). We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

To make a request, write to info@jmnii.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

12. Complaints

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Union, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner’s Office (UK) or the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Netherlands). We would, however, appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

13. Children

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

14. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
