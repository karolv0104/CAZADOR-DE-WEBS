Who we are

This website is operated by JMNII, a privately held global holding company headquartered in Miami, Florida, with an international presence in London, England and the Netherlands (“JMNII”, “we”, “us”). JMNII is responsible for the personal information described in this notice.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to share with us through the contact form on this website:

  • your full name, company, email address and country;
  • the type of inquiry you select and the content of your message.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send, sensitive personal information or confidential commercial details in an initial message.

How we use it

We use this information solely to review and respond to your inquiry and, where relevant, to continue the conversation you have started with us. We do not use it for marketing, we do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Legal basis

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we process this information on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries addressed to us, or to take steps at your request before entering into a business relationship.

We rely on carefully selected service providers to host this website and to receive form submissions on our behalf. They may process your information only on our instructions. This website also loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives your device’s IP address in order to deliver the font files.

International transfers

Because our leadership operates across the United States, the United Kingdom and the Netherlands, your information may be accessed in more than one of these countries. Where information protected by UK or EU law is transferred, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognised by that law.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information only for as long as is necessary to handle your inquiry and any relationship that follows from it, and then delete it.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores a single technical setting in your browser’s session storage so that the opening animation is shown only once per visit; this setting is removed when you close the browser tab.

Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. To make a request, please write to info@jmnii.com or use the contact form on this website. If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Union, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner’s Office (UK) or the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Netherlands).
